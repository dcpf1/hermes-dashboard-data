How it works

An 18-model weather ensemble estimates each city's daily high. A probability model turns that forecast into an odds for every temperature bucket, and the bot only takes a position when its own probability diverges enough from the market's price to clear a fixed edge. Self-improving layers score every city and quietly retire the ones that stop working. It runs 24/7 and has traded with real capital since June 16, 2026. Results here are updated hourly, automatically.
